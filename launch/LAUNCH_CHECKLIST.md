# Transparent token launch checklist

Finalize name, symbol and artwork. Publish a plain-language purpose/disclosure. Declare creator wallet(s). Define supply and distribution. Avoid hidden mint/admin powers. Avoid coordinated artificial volume or fake holders. Keep marketing claims factual. Record the launch transaction and configuration. Paper-test before risking capital. Use a separate risk budget.

The launch module should prepare configuration and validation; signing and broadcasting a transaction should remain an explicit user action.
