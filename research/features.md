# JARVIS Feature Engine

Market: volume/liquidity, transaction velocity, buy/sell imbalance, liquidity, momentum and age.
On-chain: top-account concentration, holder growth, failed transaction rate, creator history, funding overlap and wallet-cluster burstiness.
Windows: 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m and 60m. Never use future observations.
Risk flags reduce confidence; they do not prove malicious intent.
